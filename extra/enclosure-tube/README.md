# Enclosure V2

> NOTE: This is an iteration on the enclosure designed by UC San Diego students available [here](https://github.com/jlab-sensing/ENTS-enclosure). Notably the board mounts to the screw portion of the enclosure rather than the static cap. This makes assembly/removal of the board easier. The original repo is a good reference for detailed assembly instructions and cost.


## Instructions

1. 3D print the *base*, *mount*, and *guide*.
1. Cut PVC tube to desired length (TBD length).
1. Use PVC primer and cement to attach both end caps.
1. Use the *guide* to drill holes in the screw off end cap.
1. Use a step drill to enlarge the holes to fit cable glands.
1. Press threaded inserts into the mount.
1. Superglue the mount to the base.
1. Superglue the base to the screw cap.
1. Install cable glands.
